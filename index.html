If B2B enterprise clients or AI labs do not purchase datasets, or if Syntrix generates zero revenue, the company is under no financial or legal obligation to provide cash-outs, fiat conversions, or market liquidity.

Speculative and Non-Guaranteed Utility

Syntrix makes zero guarantees that Synx will ever hold external monetary value, be listable on public crypto exchanges, or be tradeable for fiat currency. Synx is an internal reward meant to be held within the platform dashboard only.

Anti-Fraud Kill Switch

Syntrix retains the absolute right to instantly terminate user profiles and permanently forfeit or burn accumulated ledger tokens if an entry triggers fraud flags (including completion speeds under 45 seconds, duplicate photo uploads, automated scripting, or gibberish inputs).

Ahmedabad Arbitration Jurisdiction

Users completely waive their rights to class-action lawsuits or court trials. All legal disputes must be settled strictly through binding individual arbitration anchored in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

2. Privacy Policy

Multi-Modal Data Collection

Syntrix collects email addresses (for account security), off-chain token ledger balances, text survey responses, uploaded document files (for OCR processing), and facial camera images (for liveness verification and biometric dataset training).

The Anonymization Fire-Wall

Personal identifiers (such as user emails and IP addresses) are isolated entirely in a secure backend database. B2B enterprise clients and AI purchasers never receive personal identities; they only acquire clean, anonymized datasets tagged by non-identifiable alphanumeric hashes.

Biometric Data Handling

Facial images collected during Selfie Mode are processed to verify liveness and anti-spoofing. Where user consent is granted, anonymized facial data is packaged into structured computer vision training datasets. Raw personal identifiers are stripped from biometric files prior to dataset compilation.

The Deletion Lock (No Data Clawbacks)

Users retain the right to terminate their accounts and delete their personal account records from the live system. However, once contributed content or survey responses have been anonymized and integrated into historical dataset bundles, those anonymized records cannot be clawed back or recalled from past sales.

Global Age Limit (18+ Only)

Access to the Syntrix protocol and data contribution modes is strictly restricted to individuals who are 18 years of age or older.
